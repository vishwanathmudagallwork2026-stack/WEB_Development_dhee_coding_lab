  1. Ravi Shastry

    About

    Ravishastry C.A. (widely referred to by students as Ravi Shastri) serves as a Director and Head of the Online Training Unit at Dhee Coding Lab, an upskilling and career placement institute based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Possessing an extensive professional background in corporate communications, market expansion, application development, and client relationship management, he plays a dual role as both an institutional leader and a hands-on technical mentor. He has been instrumental in scaling the company from its foundational roots into a prominent hub for corporate placement and technical training.

    As an educator, Shastri specializes in breaking down complex technologies for beginners. He frequently conducts intensive technical bootcamps, such as the Frontend Development Housefull Sessions, designed to equip fresh graduates with practical industry frameworks like React. His teaching approach focuses heavily on bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world execution, enabling students from diverse non-technical backgrounds to master core front-end development concepts.

    Beyond classroom instruction, Shastri plays an active role in student outreach and recruitment through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) talent drives. He acts as a primary link between regional engineering colleges and the academy, personally screening candidates and scouting talent for the institute's specialized programs.

    Graduates frequently attribute their interview confidence and technical competence to his persistent mentorship. Student testimonials highlight his structured guidance as a major factor in helping them secure positions at competitive companies like Solera, Agile Robots, and HiNeva. His unique combination of startup leadership, strategic business experience, and a transparent, collaborative mentoring style defines his significant contribution to the learning community at Dhee Coding Lab.
